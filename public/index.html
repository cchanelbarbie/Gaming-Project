How to play

01 · InviteCreate a room and share its link or code. Your friend joins on their own device.

02 · Take turnsBlue starts the first battle; the starter alternates on rematches. Select your unit, then an adjacent square. Each turn is one move or attack. No diagonal actions.

03 · Win the battleOccupy the enemy HQ (★) or eliminate all three enemy units. Each unit has 2 health. Attacks deal 1 damage; defeating a unit clears its square but does not move your attacker. Both players accept rematches.
